Hugo Spritz

Serves: 4
Cook Time: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 ice cubes
  • 125 ml soda water
  • 125 ml prosecco
  • 50 ml elderflower liqueur
  • 1 lime, cut to wedges
  • 8 mint leaves, rubbed

Method

Add ice cubes, soda water, prosecco, elderflower liqueur to a glass.

Squeeze and add the lime wedges.

Rub the mint leaves and add.

Mix
