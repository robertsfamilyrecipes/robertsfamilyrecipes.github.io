Mariano's Tomato Sauce

Serves: 8
Cook Time: 1 hour

From a site which no longer exists. https://marianos.capitalfinemeats.ca/cooking-with-marianos/marianos-tomato-sauce/

Ingredients

  • 1 220g pack bacon, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • bunch spring onion, chopped
  • 2 carrot, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato puree
  • 500g beef mince
  • 500g pork mince
  • salt and pepper
  • 3 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • parsley, chopped
  • basil, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp rosemary
  • 1tsp chili flakes

Method

Heat pan to medium. Add bacon and fry until fat renders. Add olive oil, onion, spring onion, carrots, celery, red pepper and soften for 10-15 min. Add garlic and tomato puree and fry for 2 min.

Heat a frying pan to hot. Add beef, pork, salt and pepper. Cook to get some brown on the meat.

Add meat to the veg. Deglaze the pan, and add to the veg.

Add tomatoes, parsley, basil, oregano, rosemary and chilli flakes. Bring to the simmer and cook for 30 min. Taste for salt.
