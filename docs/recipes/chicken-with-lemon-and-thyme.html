Chicken with lemon and thyme

Serves: 4
Cook Time: 1 hour

By Gary Rhodes, from an unknown magazine.

Ingredients

  • 25g butter
  • olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 500g new potatoes
  • 250g baby carrots
  • 1 chicken, cut into breast, wing, leg and thigh
  • salt and pepper
  • 8 sprigs thyme
  • 1 bay lead
  • 1 garlic clove, peeled
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 150ml double cream
  • 200g mushrooms
  • 250g green beans

Method

Heat casserole dish to medium. Add oil, butter, onions and soften for a few min. Add carrots, potatoes and fry some more.

Heat a pan to hot. Add oil, chicken, salt. Turn heat to medium and brown for a few minutes. Deglaze the pan with water and reserve. You will probably have to do two batches.

Add garlic, thyme, bay to the onions and fry for a moment. Then add stock, lemon zest and juice. Return chicken to pan. Bring to boil and cover.

Cook in oven for 40 min on 200°C fan.

Fry mushroom in pan on high heat until well cooked. Add to the casserole with 10 min to go on the cooking.

Divide chicken and veg between 4 plates and keep warm. Add bean to the juice, and reduce juice by half on a high heat. Take off heat and stir in cream. Ladle over the chicken.
