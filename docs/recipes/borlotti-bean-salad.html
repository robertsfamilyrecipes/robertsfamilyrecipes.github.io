Borlotti Bean Salad

Serves: 4 as a side
Cook Time: 30 min

From Giallo Zafferano.

Ingredients

  • 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 100g shallots, sliced
  • 3 spring thyme
  • salt and pepper
  • olive oil
  • 1 tin borlotti beans, drained and rinsed
  • 9 basil leaves

Method

To an oven tray add tomatoes, shallots, thyme, olive oil, salt, pepper, and mix. Cook in oven on 180°C fan for 20 min.

Add cooked veg, basil, olive oil to the beans and mix. Taste for salt.
