Bacon and Brie Sandwich

Serves: 2
Cook Time: 45 min

Ingredients

  • Caramelised Onions
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • Sandwich
  • 250g bacon
  • 125g brie, sliced
  • 4 slices bread

Method

Onions. Heat a pan to medium. Add oil, onions, salt and saute until golden. Add sugar, vinegar and caramelise. The whole thing takes 30min.

Bacon. Grill the bacon till crispy.

Sandwich. Butter the bread. Add bacon, onions and brie.

Heat frying pan to medium. Add oil, sandwich and fry for a few min. Flip, add more oil, and fry some more.
