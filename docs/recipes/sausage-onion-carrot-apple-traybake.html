Sausage Onion Carrot Apple Traybake

Serves: 4
Cook Time: 1 hour

From HelloFresh

Ingredients

  • 1kg new potatoes, halved
  • 2 red onion, quartered
  • 2 400g pack sausage
  • 1 carrot, batons
  • 1 apple, cored and sliced
  • Sauce
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 25g butter
  • 1 chicken stock pot

Method

Put potatoes, onion, salt and pepper on a tray and cook in oven on 180°C for 50 min. After 20 min add the sausage, apple and carrot.

Sauce. On a low heat caramelize the onion in butter. This usually takes 30-40min. Add stock pot and a little water and whisk into a sauce.
