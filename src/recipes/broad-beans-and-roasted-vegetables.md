Adapted from [Antonio Carluccio's Italian Feast](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Antonio-Carluccios-Italian-Feast-Inspired/dp/0563371692/)

### Ingredients

* 1 tin broad beans, drained
* 2 courgettes, sliced
* 1 aubergine, diced
* 1 red onion, wedged
* 1 red pepper, in strips
* 250g mushrooms, quartered
* _Marinade_
* 6 tbsp olive oil
* &frac12; garlic clove, mashed
* 1 tbsp parsley, finely chopped
* &frac12; lemon, juice
* salt

### Method

Whisk the marinade ingredients together.

Prepared the veg (not the beans), and pour over the marinade and mix.

Cremate in oven on 200&deg;C for 30-40 min.

Let cool a little, then mix in the broad beans and taste for salt.
