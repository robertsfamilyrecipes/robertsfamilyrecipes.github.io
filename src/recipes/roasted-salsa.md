General principals of making salsa by [Cook Smarts](https://www.cooksmarts.com/articles/pepper-types-and-easy-salsa-recipes/).  This version adapted from [frosted tips](https://www.bbc.co.uk/food/recipes/tortilla_towers_65545).

### Ingredients

* 6 tomatoes, halved
* 1 red onion, cut to wedges
* 2 green chillies, halved and seeded
* 4 garlic cloves, peeled
* 1 tsp chipotle flakes
* 2 limes, juiced
* salt and pepper.

### Method

Heat grill to hot.  Put tomatoes, onion, chillies, garlic on a tray and brush with olive oil. Grill for 10-15 min, until the skins char.

Liquidate roasted ingredients with chipotle flakes and lime juice.  Taste for salt.
