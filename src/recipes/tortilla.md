A family recipe from Itziar and Richard.

### Ingredients

* 4 potatoes, peeled and sliced
* 1 onion, sliced
* 1 green pepper, sliced
* 7 eggs
* olive oil
* salt

### Method

On medium heat fry potato, onion, green pepper, salt with plenty of olive oil.  This takes quite a while.  Taste for salt.

Whisk eggs in large bowl, then add the potato mix and stir round to coat.

Heat a bit more olive oil, pour in the egg/potato mix, and try till cooked on one side.

Use a plate to help you flip the tortilla.  Add a bit more oil to the pan, and cook the other side.
