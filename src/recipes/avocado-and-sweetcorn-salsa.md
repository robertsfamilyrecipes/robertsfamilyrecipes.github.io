From [Ainsley's friends and family cookbook](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Ainsley-Harriotts-Friends-Family-Cookbook/dp/0563487569/).

### Ingredients

* 1 lime, juiced
* 1 avocado, peeled, stoned and diced
* 200g tin sweetcorn, drained
* 2 tomato, chopped
* 4 spring onions, chopped
* jalapeno chillies, from jar
* salt

### Method

Juice lime into bowl, then prepare avocado adding to the lime, to stop browning.

Add the rest of the ingredients and mix.  Taste for salt.
