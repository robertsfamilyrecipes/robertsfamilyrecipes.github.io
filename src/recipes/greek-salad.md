From [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/greek-salad)

### Ingredients

* 4 tomatoes, eigthed and salted
* 1 cucumber, peeled, seeded and chopped
* &frac12; red onion, thinly sliced
* 16 black olives
* 100g feta cheese, diced
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* 4 tbsp olive oil

### Method

Prepare the ingredients into a bowl.  Drizzled over olive oil and sprinkle with thyme.  Mix.  Taste for salt.
