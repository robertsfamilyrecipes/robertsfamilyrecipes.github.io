From [Giallo Zafferano](https://www.giallozafferano.com/recipes/fresh-borlotti-bean-salad.html).

### Ingredients

* 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
* 100g shallots, sliced
* 3 spring thyme
* salt and pepper
* olive oil
* 1 tin borlotti beans, drained and rinsed
* 9 basil leaves

### Method

To an oven tray add tomatoes, shallots, thyme, olive oil, salt, pepper, and mix.  Cook in oven on 180&deg;C fan for 20 min.

Add cooked veg, basil, olive oil to the beans and mix.  Taste for salt.
